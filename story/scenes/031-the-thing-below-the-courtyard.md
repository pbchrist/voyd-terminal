# The Thing Below the Courtyard

You reach the cistern first and heave its stone lid aside.

"Don't."

The stray speaks behind you and from inside the shaft at once.

There is no water below. There is a night sky turning around a black center, though Faelspire's three suns blaze over the courtyard.

The stray grabs your shoulder. "Close it."

A shape falls upward through the stars.

Your fragment burns.

*Ask him what he threw in.*

His face answers before his mouth does.

"Twelve years ago," you say. "You put something down there."

The shape grows into a kit. One ear is torn. A black line runs down its neck exactly where the stray's scar lies.

It opens orange eyes and smiles.

"You took your time."

The stray staggers back.

You understand: he did not find a fragment twelve years ago. He escaped from one. The kit is what did not escape with him—or what the Voyd learned to wear after he ran.

The scar beneath his ear splits.

The kit's face changes with it. For one heartbeat it wears the stray's younger face: the self that entered, not the cat who fled. Your look-up has given the buried record a movement to replay, and the skin answers by making that lost self visible.

The stray clamps a paw over the split. "Do not call that thing me."

Below, the kit reaches for the rim.

"If it gets out," he says, "it will remember being me."

"And if you close it," the kit replies, "he never gets to remember being whole."

The fourth bell rings from the cistern. Cracks race across the impossible sky, and through them something vast moves above Faelspire.

The kit raises one paw. Your fragment turns toward it, ready to copy the movement.
