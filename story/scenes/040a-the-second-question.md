# The Second Question

The high door rings.

Every copy of you flinches.

Not together. In sequence.

The first jerks backward. The next follows a heartbeat later. Then the next, and the next, until your own movement from Gistli Square travels around the semicircle: shoulders recoiling, paw lifting, eyes leaving the black thing on the stones.

You remember the instant.

The fragment had spoken into the place where your next thought should have been.

You had looked away.

P'taxx stares at the copies.

“The door kept that?”

The oldest you answers without looking at him.

“It kept the turn.”

Behind the stone, the filed fragment opens its orange seam. Light pushes through the carved word **FILED** and lays an image across the floor.

You, in Gistli Square.

The fragment by your paw.

Your head beginning to turn.

The image stops one heartbeat before you look away.

The voice enters your next thought again.

*Will you do it twice?*

Your body tries.

Your neck tightens. Your eyes pull toward P'taxx, toward the copies, toward anything except the light.

You hold your gaze on the fragment.

The ring breaks in the middle of its note.

The image on the floor cannot finish.

A crack runs through **FILED**.

P'taxx takes one step back. “That is what it wanted.”

“No,” says the oldest you. “That is what it needed.”

It points to the unfinished image.

“The first time, it startled you into looking away. Then it kept the proof. Every time you obey the flinch, it adds another line.”

The oldest copy speaks your name.

The door answers by carving it beneath **FILED**.

Behind you, the stone arch that led back to the shop closes without a sound.

The test failed.

The file still found its subject.

## ◉ ACTIVE FRONTIER

The fragment provoked your first look-away and preserved it as proof. You refused the second, leaving the replay unfinished—but the ledger has identified you by name, and the room has removed the exit. The next move must decide whether to destroy the proof, falsify it, or force the door to file an act it cannot complete.
