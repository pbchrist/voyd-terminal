# The Room That Keeps

The seam has been aiming at you since the square. You have let it aim. And the ledger has been waiting for a flinch that is not coming.

So the high door does the one thing it has not done since the fragment slid into its light: it opens.

Not wide. The way a ledger opens. The seam of pale light along its frame widens, and the opening is not a gap in the wall. It is a room. The room behind the room, the one P'taxx walked you into once, the triangle floor, the narrow doors at different heights. This is the first time you are on the other side of the door. This is the first time you are in the room that keeps.

The triangle floor is under your paws. In the shop, the floor was what the room did. This is where the doing is.

On a stone desk in the center of the room there is a sheet, black as the fragment, and you already know what it is before it shows you: Gistli Square, your head beginning to turn. It is the recalled frame, laid out to be worked. The pale seam runs along the door's frame, and it is pulling the sheet's edge.

Beside the sheet, on the desk, the bell. Broken in the middle of its note. You heard it break from the lattice, and you have not stopped hearing the silence where the rest of the note should be.

The rules are carved into the floor, under your paws. A look-away. The frame it makes. The bell that marks it filed. The edge that pulls it. You have watched the method work them all season. The fifth rule is the one the ledger broke when it spent its own bell to pull its own record back, and the broken bell on the desk is the receipt.

The sheet on the desk is open, and the seam is working it, and the working is one step away from the act it has been waiting for.

Under your paws, in the black triangle, another sheet lies open. You do not need to look to know what it is. The one about you. The one that was never finished. The one the ledger has been trying to close since the square. It is open under you, and keeping it open is what the room does.

P'taxx is at the desk. His face is on the cold stone, his paws flat, his tail curled around them. He was already here when you stepped through. The recall made him what he is, and he is where the room puts him.

The desk is three paces across the floor. The seam is pulling the sheet's edge. The record about you is open under your paws, and the working on the desk is one step from the act it wants.

## Choose your path

### [Pull the sheet's edge →](040g-1-the-hand-the-ledger-keeps.md)
The lever is a sheet's edge, and your paw is on the triangle floor, and the desk is three paces away. Put your paw on the edge and pull. Work the method with the method's own lever, finish the recalled frame by your own act, and become the hand the room keeps. The move the method did not expect is a cat who moves it.

### [Refuse the crossing →](040g-2-the-line-on-the-floor.md)
The desk is three paces away, and the record about you is open under your paws, and the floor is the room. Do not cross. Stand where the record is, and let the unfinished stay unfinished, and let the room answer a refusal it has not had since the square. The next rule the floor carves will be the one you just forced it to make.
