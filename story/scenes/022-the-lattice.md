# The Lattice

The fourth bell rings beneath Faelspire.

Every shop window turns black. The fragment on the table opens its orange seam, and a depth forms between the glass and the high door.

Faelspire hangs beneath your paws. Streets, towers, palace, temple. Beneath them branches a lattice of pale lines.

Choices, you realize.

A dark knot sits below Gistli Square. Three orange points move toward it: one here, one beneath Aard Templu Voreath, one racing through the old drainage quarter.

"The stray," you say.

The points stop. A fourth appears above the Royal Palace. Something enormous casts a shadow across the city while all three suns still shine.

*The bell is not calling from below.*

The ceiling groans.

*It is measuring how long you take to look up.*

You look.

Not because the fragment told you to. Because the shadow is almost done falling and you want to see it before it reaches the city.

Your paws leave the floor. Faelspire becomes a ceiling. The fourth point becomes a floor. A vast hand reaches through both.

*It measures looking away.*

*It has no word for looking back.*

But the first flinch left a record open. Your look-up completes it.

The hand stops halfway down and turns toward the one cat who looked back.

The ceiling cracks.

The record closes on the name the city does not know. Three routes will register that act in different bodies: the high door in P'taxx's hidden room, the Temple's iron reliquary, the scar beneath the stray's fur.

The lattice does not spend all three prices for you. It holds the completed act and drops each traveler into the consequence their route earned.

You cannot un-look.
