# The Cost of Holding

The recall has landed. The filed frame is laid across the stone, and the line beneath **FILED** is carved, and the copy stands where you left it, eyes on the recalled frame, a subject now.

The ring is broken. The ledger has paid. And the cost of the recall is not the ring.

The cost is P'taxx.

His paw is still on your shoulder. It has been since the room formed, since the seam aimed, since the copies held their positions. His paw is warm, and his breath is shallow, and his face is the face of a cat who has been standing with you through every refused flinch since the square, and his face is not looking at the seam.

His face is looking at you.

Not at the copy. Not at the recalled frame. Not at the line beneath **FILED**. At you. And the look is not the look of an ally. It is the look of a subject.

The ledger did not just recall the frame. It reached back through the keeping, and the keeping was not only the frame. The keeping was the record of every moment you stood with P'taxx in this room, every moment his paw was on your shoulder, every moment you refused and he stood with you. And the recall pulled all of it back, and the recall made all of it a subject.

P'taxx's paw pulls back.

Not quickly. Not in fear. The way a paw pulls back from a paw it has decided not to hold. His face stays on your face, and the look stays, and the look is the look of a cat who has just seen, in the ledger's light, what you are: not a witness, not a cat who refuses, but a subject. The record about you is unfinished, but the record about you standing with him is not. The ledger finished that one. The ledger finished the relationship.

The copies shift.

They do not move. They do not flinch. They stand in their semicircle, and their faces are your face, and their eyes are on the recalled frame, and the look is not a cat's look. It is a subject's look. They are all subjects now, the ledger's light having passed through the room and found, in every face that is your face, the shape of a subject.

The seam turns.

Not away from you. Around you. The light on the floor swings, and you feel it take you the way it took the copy — not the frame, the name. Your name. The one the door carved beneath **FILED** in the second question. The record about you is the one that was never finished, and you have just refused the reading, and the cost of the refusal is not the lever. The cost of the refusal is the cat who stood with you while you refused.

The exit is still closed.

The name is still filed.

The copy stands where you left it, eyes on its recalled frame, and the ledger works. And P'taxx stands where he was, facing the door, his paw at his side, his face on your face, and the face is not the face of an ally. The face is the face of a subject. And the subject is the one the ledger is finishing.

You did not want to be measured. You did not want to be the subject of a reading you did not choose to be in. And you have just refused the reading, and the refusal holds, and the record stands unfinished, and the cost of the refusal is the one cat in this room who was not a subject until the ledger made him one.

Not quiet. Still.
