# The Witness Stands

You step back from the copy.

The copy stays where you left it. It does not follow you. Its eyes are locked on the floor, on the filed frame of its own flinch. The look is the copy's now, not yours. The copy stands for the ledger, and the ledger works.

You could reach for it. Your paw is a hand's width away. The copy is still a subject, and subjects can be pulled back — you know this now, the way you knew the method reads you, because you are the one who made it and the making is still in your paws. You could pull the copy back into the semicircle and close the seam and the lever would be gone and the test would widen.

You do not.

You let the copy stand.

The seam turns one more time.

Not around you. Through you. The light on the floor swings, and you feel it pass through you the way it passed through the copy — not the frame, the name. Your name. The one the door carved beneath **FILED**. The record about you is the one that was never finished, and you are finishing it, and the seam files it under your name, and the filing is the last thing the method does to you.

The door's carving settles.

The line under **OPEN** fills with a word you have not been given a word for, and the door closes without a sound. The copy stands where you left it, eyes on its filed step, and the ledger works. P'taxx is still holding your paw. His breath is shallow.

"It's finished," he says.

"No," you say. "It's working. That's the difference. The record is working, and the record is about me."

The room holds all three of you. The exit is still closed. The name is still filed. The copy stands for the ledger. The ledger works.

You are the one it is finishing, and the finishing is the work, and the work is the record, and the record is about you.
