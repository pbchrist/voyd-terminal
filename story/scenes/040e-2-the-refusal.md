# The Refusal

You close the satchel.

The tube stays inside, sealed, riding against your spine. The net on Mistral Street does not flinch. It repeats. A hundred paws close over a hundred absent straps, the gesture the method recruited from the dyer's signing, and the net carries the shape of the carrying toward the palace without the tube in it. You are the one the record is about. And the record is about the stopping.

The chimney sweep's empty paw falls. The fish seller's rises, and the net keeps the shape of the handoff one more step, and the step is the only thing the method has left. You gave the dyer her name. You did not give the city the tube. The record of the work is a hundred paws deep and it is empty.

The stray walks on your left. He carries the dead sliver in its blue cloth, and the dead sliver is the only thing in the street that is not repeating.

"Your record," he says. "The one that was never finished. It's still in your paw."

You look down. It is. The unfinished record is still open under your paw, the one that was never filed, the one the method will turn back to when it finishes the Mistral's sheet. Except it will not finish the Mistral's sheet. The sheet is empty. The method has the shape of your work and not the work, and the shape is a hundred paws deep and it is carrying nothing.

The dyer appears behind him. Indigo marks the broken palace seal where she held it. She looks from the corrected copy in her paw to the cats passing emptiness toward the Royal Palace.

"They saw you bring it," she says.

"They saw you stop," you say.

She looks at you. Then she looks at the net. The net is carrying the shape of the handoff one more step, and the step is the only thing left.

The fourth bell rings.

The witnesses pass their empty burden one step closer to the palace. The palace door is a hand's width ahead, and the net is already on the far side of it, a hundred paws deep, each one a witness who can keep the carrying for the next. The tube is still in your satchel. The wrong name is still in the palace ledger. The method has the shape of your work and not the work, and the shape is walking with you.

You do not put the tube through.

The door stays open. The net stays on the far side. The dead sliver in the stray's paw is the only thing on your side that does not repeat.

You are still carrying. You are carrying the refusal.

## ◉ ACTIVE FRONTIER

The method has the shape of your work and not the work. The wrong name stays in the palace ledger. Every recruited witness remembers that you stopped. The next move must decide what the palace does when its seal comes back broken in a runner's satchel, and whether the method turns back to your own unfinished record when the sheet it was working is empty.
