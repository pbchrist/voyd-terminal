# The Filing

The lattice drops you back onto the shop floor.

P'taxx is the only one here.

There is no procession. No temple cords, no blades, no young acolyte whispering that this is impossible. There is P'taxx, and the copies, and the room behind the room, and the high door.

The copies are where the lattice left them. They stand in the semicircle the way they have since the corridor, in the clothes of six ages of Faelspire. Every one of them has your face. One carries a broken crown. One has blood on both forepaws. One is old enough that your face has collapsed into angles you have never imagined wearing.

They do not move when the lattice drops you down. They are watching the high door.

The high door is open.

Not wide. Open the way a ledger opens, along the seam of pale light that widened when the fragment slid into it, when you watched the light close, when the title shifted to **FILED**. The fragment is behind the door. The proof is behind the door. And the proof is yours.

You look at your paw.

The linen is empty. You remember it empty. You remember P'taxx saying *the door keeps what it reads*, and you remember the new line beneath the old title, and you do not need him to read it aloud for the second time.

**YOU LOOKED AWAY.**

The Lattice is showing you the fourth point now.

It is not a hand. Not a shadow. It is the door, and the door is the point, and the point is the one that stopped when you looked up. The ring the door makes is the fourth point's voice, and the ring is the sound the Lattice is measuring, and the Lattice is the lattice of pale lines beneath the city, and the knot beneath Gistli Square is the place where the ring first started.

The ring breaks in the middle of its note.

A crack runs through **FILED**.

The image on the floor is you, in Gistli Square, head beginning to turn, stopped one heartbeat before the look-away. The image does not finish. It cannot finish. You held it. You held it with the city watching, and the city is watching still, and the lattice is the city, and the lattice is the one that is finishing the record about you.

P'taxx's paw finds your shoulder.

“You held it,” he says.

“It is the same test,” you say.

“It is the same test,” he says. “Asked at a higher price.”

The Lattice is waiting.

The high door is open. The filed frame is laid across the stone. The line beneath **FILED** is being carved. And the copy that was never yours is standing between you and the opened seam, the one copy that did not flinch when the door rang, the one that stepped out of the semicircle and held its ground, and the Lattice is showing you that the copy's standing was not a file, and the copy's eyes were not a mirror.

The copy is a subject.

The seam is opened. The line is being finished. The city is watching. The high door is open. The copy is standing. The seam is aiming. The line is being carved. The subject is the copy. The record about you is the one that was never finished. The Lattice is the one that is finishing it.

The name is filed.

The name is not on the fragment. The name is on the door. The fragment was the lever. The name is the cost. And the cost is not a thing you can walk back from. It is a thing that stays.
