# The Act Not Narrated

You do not cross.

The seam is aiming. The word **OPEN** is held. The copy stands in the middle of the aim, and the light is laid across the floor, and the voice is waiting in the space the copy made. And you do not cross. You do not put your shoulder against its shoulder. You do not let your eyes fall into the method's angle. You stand where you are, with P'taxx's paw halfway to your shoulder and the copies holding their positions, and you let the seam look at you and you let it not find what it is looking for.

The act is not narrated.

It is the act you chose. Not the act the voice described. Not the act the room expected. The act is yours, and it is the one that does not move.

The seam reads you.

Not quiet. Still.

The light on the floor stays aimed at you, the way a held breath stays aimed at the mouth that will not give it air. The word under **OPEN** does not fill. The carving waits.

The copy's eyes lift.

They are not looking at you. They are looking at the seam, wary and full, the look of a cat that has just been told it is no longer the one being tested. The copy stands where you left it, and it looks at the seam, and the seam looks back, and neither of them moves.

The record about the copy is not complete. The flinch is not filed under its name. It stays a witness — a cat standing between you and the seam, not a subject the ledger can work on.

The record about you is not complete.

You did not flinch. The method did not get its second frame. The image on the floor — the one that could not finish — stays unfinished, and the seam has no word for a cat that looks at it and does not move.

You do not want to be measured. You do not want to be the subject of a reading you did not choose to be in. And you have just refused the reading, and the cost is the lever. The copy stays a witness — a cat standing between you and the seam, not a subject the ledger can work on — and a witness you cannot deploy is a lever you no longer have. The only move that could have made the ledger see its own error is gone, because you did not make it.

P'taxx's paw closes on your shoulder.

“It's finished,” he says. “The record. It's finished.”

“No,” you say. “It's unfinished. That's the point.”

The room holds you: the cat who was never finished, the cat who is a witness, and the seam that has nothing to file. The exit is still closed. The name is still filed. The copy stands where you left it, eyes on the seam, and the ledger waits for a flinch that is not coming.

The act is not narrated. It is held. And holding it is a different act than the one the voice described, and the different act is the one that costs the lever, and the cost of the lever is the only thing in this room that the ledger cannot work on, and the ledger is waiting for a flinch that is not coming, and the flinch is not coming, and the not-coming is the act you chose, and the act you chose is the one that is not narrated.
