# The Crossing Question

The lattice does not wait for you to land.

You are still falling through the city-ceiling when the Lattice's fourth point stops, and the stop is not a pause. It is a door.

Above the Royal Palace, the hand that was reaching down through the city halts. The fingers close. They open. They close again, slowly, and the closing is a ring, and the ring is the sound your filed door makes in the room you left behind.

*The high door rings.*

You have not thought the words. The Lattice thinks them for you, in the space between you and the shadow above, and the shadow above knows them, and the shadow above is listening.

Below you, in the lattice's pale lines, the dark knot beneath Gistli Square holds still. One orange point moves out of it. Not toward the knot. Away. It travels the distance the room is from the city in a single beat, and it arrives at a high door, and the door opens, and the light that pushes through the opening lays an image across the stone floor of the room you left behind.

You, in Gistli Square.

The fragment by your paw.

Your head beginning to turn.

The image stops one heartbeat before you look away.

You have seen this frame before. It is the frame you filed. You watched it slide into the high door's light, you watched the light close, you watched the title shift to **FILED**. You chose that. You chose to keep the proof, and the proof kept you, and now the Lattice has carried the proof back into your sight, and the city above is watching you watch it.

The voice enters your next thought.

*Will you do it twice?*

Your body tries.

Your neck tightens. The city is a ceiling now, and the fourth point is a floor, and the light from the high door is laid across the stone beneath you, and your eyes pull toward P'taxx, toward the acolytes, toward the young acolyte whose face you can see even now, toward anything except the image on the floor of the room you left behind.

The shadow above is waiting.

The Lattice is waiting.

You hold your gaze on the filed frame.

The high door's ring breaks in the middle of its note.

The image on the floor cannot finish.

A crack runs through **FILED**.

P'taxx, in the room below, takes one step back.

"You held it," he says. "With the city watching. That is not the same test."

"It is the same test," you say.

The oldest you speaks from the room you left behind, and the Lattice carries the voice up through the lattice, and the shadow above hears it too.

"It is the same test, asked at a higher price."

The high door answers.

It does not carve a name. It does not carve a word. It carves a line beneath **FILED**, and the line is not a crack, and the line is not a frame, and the line is the one the Lattice's fourth point left on the city when it stopped.

The Lattice changes.

The dark knot beneath Gistli Square is no longer a knot. It is a subject. The orange point that was your filed proof has moved into it, and the point is no longer orange. It is the color of the line beneath **FILED**, and the line is the color of the subject, and the subject is the one the Lattice is finishing.

The shadow above the Royal Palace turns its hand away from the city.

Not back. Away. Toward the knot. Toward the subject. Toward the room you left behind, where the high door is still open, and the filed frame is still laid across the stone, and the line beneath **FILED** is still being carved, and the copy that was never yours is standing between you and the opened seam.

The Lattice shows you the copy.

It was never in the Lattice. It was in the room, the whole time, the one copy that did not flinch when the door rang, the one copy that had been watching P'taxx, the one that had stepped out of the semicircle and stood between you and the opened seam, and the Lattice is showing you that the copy's step was not a flinch, and the copy's standing was not a file, and the copy's eyes were not a mirror.

The copy is a subject.

The line beneath **FILED** fills with a word you have not been given a word for.

The shadow above the Royal Palace stops reaching.

It does not fade. It holds, with its hand turned away from the city and aimed at the knot, and the knot is the room, and the room is the high door, and the high door is the one that filed you, and the line beneath **FILED** is the one the Lattice is finishing, and the copy is the one standing between you and the opened seam, and the seam is the one the Lattice is showing you, and the Lattice is the one the city is watching, and the city is the one the shadow is aimed at, and the shadow is the one that stopped, and the stop is the door, and the door is the one that rang, and the ring is the one that broke, and the break is the one that was not finished.

The test failed.

The file found its subject.

The subject is the copy.

The copy is between you and the opened seam.

The seam is opened.

The line is being finished.

The city is watching.

The high door is still open.

The copy is still standing.

The seam is still aiming.

The line is still being carved.

The subject is still the copy.

The record about you is the one that was never finished.

The Lattice is the one that is finishing it.
