# The Crossing Question

The broken ring catches you before your first landing can settle. Pale lines close around your ribs and pull. The shop floor falls away a second time.

Above the Royal Palace, the hand reaching down through the city halts. It closes into a ring. The ring is the sound your filed door makes in the room you left behind.

*The high door rings.*

You have not thought the words. The Lattice thinks them for you, in the space between you and the shadow above. The shadow is listening.

Below you, in the lattice's pale lines, the dark knot beneath Gistli Square holds still. One orange point moves out of it, away from the knot. It travels the distance from room to city in a single beat and arrives at a high door. The door opens. The light that pushes through the opening lays an image across the stone floor of the room you left behind.

You, in Gistli Square.

The fragment by your paw.

Your head beginning to turn.

The image stops one heartbeat before you look away.

You have seen this frame before. It is the frame you filed. You watched it slide into the high door's light, watched the light close, watched the title shift to **FILED**. You chose that. You chose to keep the proof, and the proof kept you. Now the Lattice has carried the proof back into your sight, and the city above is watching you watch it.

The voice enters your next thought.

*Will you do it twice?*

Your body tries. Your neck tightens. The city is a ceiling now, and the fourth point is a floor, and your eyes pull toward P'taxx, toward the copies, toward anything except the image on the floor of the room you left behind.

You hold your gaze on the filed frame.

The high door's ring breaks in the middle of its note. The image on the floor cannot finish. A crack runs through **FILED**.

P'taxx, in the room below, takes one step back. "You held it," he says. "With the city watching. That is not the same test."

"It is the same test," you say.

The oldest you speaks from the room you left behind, and the Lattice carries the voice up through the lattice. "It is the same test, asked at a higher price."

The high door answers. It carves a line beneath **FILED**. The line is the one the Lattice's fourth point left on the city when it stopped.

The Lattice changes. The dark knot beneath Gistli Square is no longer a knot. It is a subject. The orange point that was your filed proof has moved into it.

The shadow above the Royal Palace turns its hand away from the city. Not back. Away. Toward the knot. Toward the room you left behind, where the high door is still open and the filed frame is still laid across the stone, and the copy that was never yours is standing between you and the opened seam.

The copy is a subject.

The test failed. The file found its subject. The subject is the copy. The copy is between you and the opened seam.
