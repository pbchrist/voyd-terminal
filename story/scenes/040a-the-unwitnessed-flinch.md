# The Unwitnessed Flinch

The filed fragment is open on the floor, and it is watching.

Not the image. The image is still you, in Gistli Square, head beginning to turn — stopped one heartbeat before the look-away. The image does not move. But the orange seam around **FILED** has widened, and the light that pushes through it has a weight to it now. A direction. It lays across the floor like a tongue testing the air, and it is aimed at you.

You have been filed.

The oldest you knows it before you do.

"The ledger has a witness," it says. "That is how it completes a record. A movement it cannot see is a movement it cannot keep."

P'taxx stares at the opened seam. "Then stop letting it see you."

The voice enters your next thought.

*Will you do it twice?*

Your body tries. Your neck tightens. Your eyes pull toward the light — toward the copies, toward P'taxx, toward anything except the filed you on the floor.

You do not look away.

You look back.

Not at the light. Not at the copies. You turn your head one full inch toward the orange seam and hold it there, on the exact frame of your own backward jerk, the frame the seam has kept, the frame that is the proof. You look at the record of your flinch. You let it see you looking at it.

The seam goes still.

The image on the floor does not finish.

A crack runs through **FILED**.

Then the second crack.

Then the third, and the light pushes back through the seam like a hand pulling something free.

One of the copies in the semicircle stops.

It is not the oldest. It is the middle one, the one that had been watching P'taxx. It turns its head toward you, and the turn is slow, and it is not a flinch, and it is not a file. It is a cat looking at you with its own eyes, and it takes one step out of the semicircle, and it stands between you and the opened seam, and it does not move.

The voice speaks again, and this time it is not in your head. It is in the space between the copy and the seam, in the space that the copy has made.

*You are the witness.*

The copy's eyes hold yours.

It did not flinch. It did not file. It stepped out of the record and into the room, and now the ledger has something it cannot complete: a witness that is also a subject, a record that is also a cat.

The door carves one more word beneath **FILED**.

**OPEN.**

The test has changed. It is no longer *will you flinch*. It is *will you let it see you not flinch*, and the witness is standing between you and the answer.
