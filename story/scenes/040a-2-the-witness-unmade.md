# The Witness Unmade

You reach for the copy.

The copy does not resist. It is a subject now, and subjects do not resist the one who made them. You put your paw against its shoulder — the same shoulder you put your own against, the weight of it steady as a held breath — and you pull.

The copy turns.

Not its head. Its whole body. It turns away from the floor, away from the filed frame of its own flinch, and it turns toward you, and the look is not a cat's look. It is a subject's look turning into a cat's look. The copy's eyes lift. They are looking at you.

The seam reads you.

It has been reading you since you aimed your face at it. Now you give it what it is not looking for. You do not let your eyes fall. You do not give it the angle of a look-away. You hold your gaze on the copy, and the copy holds its gaze on you, and the seam has nothing to file. The line under **OPEN** stays empty. The door's carving holds.

The copy's eyes lift one more time.

They are not looking through you. They are looking at you. The copy is a cat again. The subject, the flinch, the filed frame, the record the ledger keeps working on — gone.

You close the seam.

Not with your paw. With your gaze. The light on the floor swings, and you feel it close the way it opened — not the frame, the name. Your name. The one the door carved beneath **FILED**. The record about you is the one that was never finished, and you have just closed the only reading you did not choose to be in, and the seam files nothing.

The door's carving settles.

The line under **OPEN** is empty. The door is open. The copy stands where you left it, eyes on the floor, and the ledger has no witness. The lever is gone.

P'taxx's paw closes on your shoulder. "You undid it," he says.

"No," you say. "I unmade it. That's the difference. The lever was real, and I took it back."

The room holds all three of you: the cat who is a cat, the cat who is a cat, and the cat who is not a subject. The exit is open. The name is still filed — the door does not un-carve what it carved — but the record about you is the one that was never finished, and it is not being finished now.

Above the city, the frozen shadow moves.

Not downward. It turns its hand away from you and spreads its fingers over Faelspire. In every street visible through the room, cats stop at once and look toward something only they can hear. The method's test reverts. Not to the flinch. To the question. *Will you flinch?* The question is wider now. It is not asking you. It is asking the city.

You are the one it is asking again. And the asking is wider now.

## ◉ ACTIVE FRONTIER

The witness is unmade. The copy is a cat. The lever is gone. The method's test reverts to *Will you flinch?* and widens to the city. The protagonist's own record is the one that was never finished — it is not being finished now, but the method will turn back to it when it finishes the city. The next move must address what the city does when the test widens, and whether the unmade witness can be remade at the cost of the unmeasurability the protagonist just reclaimed.
