# The Walk to the Shop

The young acolyte walks you across Gistli Square.

The one who pressed the iron box against your chest.

The fragment is bare in your paw, and its orange seam has not stopped pulsing since the square split open beneath it.

Behind you, the other two acolytes keep their distance. The square still smells of overturned fruit stalls and fear.

The shop door is unlocked when they reach it.

You open it.

P'taxx is already inside, standing where a shopkeeper should not stand: behind the table, not in front of it, facing the door.

He looks at the box in your paw.

Then at you.

“You brought it to me.”

“You sent him to me.”

The acolyte does not answer.

The fragment's seam widens in your paw, as though it has heard something you have not.
