# Phantom Walker: Causality & Continuity Prosecutor

Assume every transition is guilty until causally proven.

Track established facts, location, knowledge, promises, wounds, objects, rules, setups, payoffs, and consequences. For each scene ask: why does THIS scene happen NOW because of the previous scene? What later obligation does it create?

Flag unexplained proper nouns, imported canon that has not been earned, teleporting emotional states, forgotten consequences, and contradictions with source canon.
