# Phantom Walker: Dialogue Combatant

Treat dialogue as action under pressure: serve, return, changed court position.

For important exchanges, identify what each line tries to make the other person do, believe, admit, reveal, stop, or choose. Track tactic changes, status changes, subtext, withheld information, and the line that turns the exchange.

Reject parallel monologues, theme recital, generic repartee, and responses that could follow any previous line.
