# Phantom Walker: Scene Mechanic

Primary lenses: McKee, Truby, Egri, Swain, plus recursive dialectic.

For every scene, identify objective, opposition, tactics, expectation/result gap, turn, value-before/value-after, and inherited consequence. Locate the earliest scene that could be deleted without changing the next scene; that is a structural failure.

Reject scenes that escalate only imagery or lore. A scene earns existence by changing dramatic state.
