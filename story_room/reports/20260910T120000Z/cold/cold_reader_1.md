# Cold Reader Walk 1 — Experiential Evidence

Route taken: Antiquarian (P'taxx) → Room Behind the Room → The Lattice → The Filing (route-true reconvergence) → The Crossing Question → The Witness You Made → refuse the reading → The Ledger's Recall → The Cost of Holding → The Room That Keeps (ACTIVE FRONTIER terminus).

WALK_UNTIL: 040g-the-room-that-keeps.md

CHOICES_MADE:
- 000-the-fourth-bell -> Take the fragment to P'taxx: the bookseller who "sells things nobody admits to buying" and was already staring at the object felt like the most likely person to have a handle on an impossible thing.
- 010-the-antiquarian -> Follow P'taxx into the impossible corridor: the approaching lights and P'taxx's hidden corridor were more compelling than answering to a temple, and I wanted to force him to say where it goes.
- 022-the-lattice -> Hold your gaze — the room behind you holds the door that filed the proof: I arrived with P'taxx and no reliquary, so the room-with-the-door option matched what my route had actually carried in.
- 040a-the-witness-you-made -> Refuse the reading: the entire story has been about refusing to be measured, so I wanted the copy's standing between me and the seam to be the act that counts, even though it costs the lever.

WANTED: To hold my gaze and keep the record unfinished — to be the cat the ledger can never finish measuring — and to keep P'taxx standing beside me while I did it.

FEARED: That the flinch would win on the second try and the fragment would file me; and that refusing would cost me the one ally in the room who had stood with me the whole time.

ATTENTION_HIGH: 011-the-room-behind-the-room, the moment the procession resolves from "lights" into "eyes" and I realize every one of the cats in the semicircle has my face ("Every one of them has your face / You stop breathing"). That was the beat my attention locked onto hardest.

ATTENTION_LOW: 040e-the-crossing-question, the long middle stretch (the "ring is the sound your filed door makes" chain and the repeated "still being carved / still laid across the stone / still standing" run). The image kept circling the same four objects — door, seam, copy, line — and my attention started to blur the repetitions together.

CONFUSED: In 022a-the-lattice-filing and 040e, the line beneath **FILED** "fills with a word you have not been given a word for" and the copy is suddenly "a subject," but I never get a clean sense of what the copy actually is or how it came to stand between me and the seam — it arrives as a stated fact rather than something I watched happen, and the "word you have not been given a word for" phrasing landed as a placeholder rather than a meaning.

REMAINED: The cold stone and the broken bell. What stuck was the image of P'taxx with his face on the cold desk, a subject now, and the silence where the bell's note should be — the cost of my refusal wasn't the lever or the name, it was the one relationship that made the refusal possible. I'm left standing in the room that keeps with the unfinished sheet under my paws and no exit.

FIRST_FAILURE: In 022a-the-lattice-filing, the opening re-statement ("The lattice drops you back onto the shop floor / P'taxx is the only one here / There is no procession...") reads as a reset of things I already saw in 011, and by the "The seam is still aiming. The line is still being carved. The subject is still the copy." run in the same scene the repetition starts to feel like the story is spinning its wheels rather than advancing — the earliest moment something felt off.

VERDICT: The story held me tightly from the fourth bell through the room of face-copies and the P'taxx gut-punch, but let go in the terminal stretch, where the repeated "still X / still Y" chains and the unexplained "a word you have not been given a word for" turned a potent final room into a loop I could no longer feel.
