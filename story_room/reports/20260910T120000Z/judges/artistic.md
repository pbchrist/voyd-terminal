## 21 — Thematic argument: 4
Competing truths dramatized, not announced: the ledger's thesis (every flinch is fileable; looking away is the unit of being) vs. the refusal (a gaze that will not move is the one unfileable act). Both embodied in action:
- 040a-03: "You let the seam look at you and you let it not find what it is looking for." Refusal as a physical non-event.
- 040f: "The cost of the refusal is not the lever. The cost of the refusal is the cat who stood with you while you refused." Refusal is not free.
- 040g: the broken bell is the system's own scar — the method pays for its own failure.
Argument stays in tension at the frontier (does the refusal hold "where the refusal is a physical act, not an attitude?"). Docked for one announced thesis: 040d "A fragment is not merely evidence that the Voyd exists. It is evidence against the cat who carried it" — told, not shown.

## STRONGEST_ARTISTIC_ELEMENT
**040g's broken bell.** "The ledger spent its only bell to tear its own record, and the bell is on the desk, broken, and the silence where the note should be is the cost the room is standing in." One object doing five jobs at once: gives the method a body; makes the system's cost physical and visible; transforms the bell from sound to wound; embodies the theme (the system that measures flinches is itself wounded by the refusal); and creates a new dramatic state (the silence is not empty — it is a standing cost). The story's strongest image, thematic statement, and dramatic turn in one object. Protect it.
