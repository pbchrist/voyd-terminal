## THE SINGLE LOAD-BEARING DIAGNOSIS

**The reconvergence architecture treats route difference as information to be re-asserted, not as causal state to be carried. The branches were written to deliver the same reveal — the lattice's fourth point, the "test," the name — so the forks are flavor toggles, not causal forks, and every scene at or downstream of a reconvergence inherits the damage.**

This is the one diagnosis that, repaired, removes the most downstream damage. The causal chain:

1. **030's cistern fork is informational-only.** Both branches converge on an *identical* downstream physical state (040c/040d: lid jammed, name legible, city silent). The choice differs only in whether the protagonist learned the twelve-years backstory. The rubric's exact failure sign applies: *a scene in the middle of the branch can be removed without changing later behavior.* The player buys "a different flavor of the same next scene" (the cold walk's own words).
2. **021b pre-spent the temple branch's distinctive state.** The temple route's signature object (the reliquary's recognition) is consumed in 021b as a teleport to P'taxx's shop where "You sent him to me" is never resolved. So when 022b re-asserts "The iron reliquary is open… Your name," it is re-asserting an object whose *causal history* was already spent elsewhere — and 022b's "You have another fragment" is a turn the route never set up. The reconvergence preserves the branch's *objects*, not its *causal history*.
3. **031's route-knowledge bleed is the same pathology at the voice level.** The Stray route's peak beat (the kit reveal) is contaminated by a line that assumes Antiquarian-route interior state ("the door in the room behind the shop… the same filed fragment"). The scene's revelation *presupposes* state this route never produced.
4. **040b/040d/040c are single-exit corridors.** The best dilemma (040a) is gated behind the least-chosen route; the causally most differentiated entry (temple) delivers a corridor. The player cannot *feel the story could have gone otherwise* on two of four routes — the exact failure sign of active law `law_a509c75fb6a1`.
5. **The "still" chains are the reconvergence's delivery mechanism.** A reconvergence node that has nothing new to say (because both branches arrived at the same state) *restates the state* to fill the gap. 022a/022b/040e/040g re-assert because the branch difference was not carried as state to assert. The chains are the *symptom*; the erasure is the *cause*.
6. **The missing role compounds it.** Because the protagonist is a want, not a life, there is no *independent* causal state for a branch to carry *about the protagonist*. Route difference can only be expressed as *what object is in the room*, not *who the cat has become*. So every reconvergence is forced to re-assert objects, and the voice fills the gaps with the "still" chains.

**Why this is the earliest/highest-leverage wound.** The first reconvergence in the corpus is 022 (all routes → lattice), and the first route-specific break is 021b (the temple teleport). Everything the room has since built — the M1 repair (route-true assertion beats), candidate law `law_4d3da1051141` (branch-awareness), the S1+S2 stack (the ledger door + look-up), the N1 selection (the witness you made) — is *band-aiding the same wound*: making reconvergence nodes *re-assert* branch state. But re-assertion is the weak fix; the genome's own candidate law (`law_4d3da1051141`) records that M2 (reader-memory) and M3 (character testimony) were *weaker* because they left "geographic residual." The corpus has demonstrated, cycle after cycle, that reconvergence is where route state breaks — and every repair has made the *reconvergence node* smarter rather than making the *branches* carry different state. The wound is not at the reconvergence. It is upstream, in the branches.

**Why it poisons future scenes.** The 040g frontier note says the next scene must address "whether a cat who has refused the reading and lost the one ally who believed in the refusal can still refuse — in the room where the refusal is a physical act, not an attitude." That question is *route-gated*: it is only fully alive on the antiquarian route (where P'taxx exists as a cost). On the temple route (040b) and the stray routes (040c/040d), the frontier note does not apply — those players have no P'taxx, no recall, no broken bell. The canonical head is the *antiquarian head*. Every other route is a corridor that reconverges onto it. If the next scene is written for the canonical head (as it must be, given the frontier note), the temple and stray routes will be *erased again* — their players will have been told a route-specific story whose conclusion was written for a different route's state. The damage will compound: the more the story extends the canonical head, the more the other routes become flavor toggles.

**Why the "still" chains are not the target.** They are the *symptom* of the reconvergence erasure. A reconvergence that carries real branch difference has *new state to assert*, and the "still" chains disappear on their own — the node no longer needs to re-describe what the audience already holds, because the audience is holding *two* things (both routes' state) and the node's job is to *collide* them, not re-read them. **Why the missing role is not the target.** It is real and important (character cat 7 = 2.5), but it is *orthogonal* to the reconvergence. ## ROOT CAUSE

**The branches were written to deliver the same reveal, so the choice was a flavor toggle, not a causal fork.**

The dramatic cause is not "the reconvergence node fails to re-assert state." That is the symptom. The cause is upstream, in the *writing intent* of the branches: each route was authored to arrive at the *same dramatic destination* (the lattice's fourth point, the "test," the name) with only the *object in the room* differing (the copy / the reliquary / the kit). The choice at 000 (and at 030) was designed to *vary the experience of arriving*, not to *vary the state arrived at*. The reconvergence was then *inevitable* — not because the story failed to carry state, but because the state was never *different* to carry.

The genome's own history confirms this: the M1 repair (cycle 20260830T180000Z) fixed 021's reconvergence break by adding *route-true assertion beats* — i.e., by making the reconvergence node *smarter*. The S1+S2 stack (cycle 20260831T030002Z) fixed the 022 convergence by adding *the ledger door + the look-up* — again, making the convergence *do more work*. The N1 selection (cycle 20260902T030001Z) fixed the 040a seam by making *the protagonist's act* the cause of the turn — which is correct and is the protected element — but it did not change the fact that the *other routes* still converge on the same state. Every cycle has repaired the *reconvergence node* rather than the *branch difference*. The wound has been treated at the wrong scale.

The root cause, stated as a dramatic fact: **the story has one engine (the protagonist's want vs. the ledger's method) and three routes that were written as three *ways of experiencing* that engine, not as three *states that the engine produces*.** The routes are *presentation variants* of a single dramatic line, not *causal divergences* of a branching dramatic line. The reconvergence is not a failure of state-carrying; it is the *expected and correct behavior* of presentation variants. The fix is not to make the reconvergence node smarter; it is to make the *branches produce different state* — so that the reconvergence has something genuinely new to collide, rather than something to re-assert.

## MUTATION TARGET

**A structural mutation that makes the 000 three-way fork (and the 030 cistern fork) produce *causally different state* that the reconvergence node then *collides* rather than re-asserts.**

The structural dimension to change: **branch consequence — specifically, the state each branch carries into the reconvergence, and the reconvergence node's job (from re-assertion to collision).**

Concretely (for the Structural Editor, not a directive):

- **The 000 fork must produce different *causal state*, not different *objects*.** Currently: antiquarian carries the filed fragment / copy; temple carries the open reliquary / name; stray carries the kit / name under the skin. These are *objects in the room*. The mutation should make each route produce a different *state of the protagonist's relation to the ledger* — e.g., different *knowledge*, different *obligation*, different *spent resource* — such that the reconvergence node has two (or three) *incompatible states* to collide, not two objects to re-assert.
- **The reconvergence node (022, and its downstream 022a/022b) must be a *collision* of branch state, not a *re-assertion* of branch objects.** The M1 repair added route-true assertion beats; the mutation should go one step further: the reconvergence node's *turn* (movement 3) must be produced by the *collision of the branch states*, not by the system's next move. The "still" chains disappear because the node has new state to assert (the collision's result), not old state to re-read.
- **The 030 cistern fork must produce different *downstream physical state*, not different *backstory knowledge*.** Currently both branches arrive at 040c/040d with identical physical state (lid jammed, name legible, city silent). The mutation should make the "open the cistern" branch and the "force the explanation" branch arrive at *different physical states* — e.g., one branch jams the lid, the other does not; one branch makes the name legible, the other does not — so that the reconvergence (if one exists) has a genuine state difference to carry, and the 040c/040d frontiers have different *available actions* (opening the single-exit corridors).
- **The temple route (020 → 021b → 022b → 040b) must not pre-spend its distinctive state in 021b.** The 021b teleport (to P'taxx's shop, "You sent him to me") should be restructured so that the temple branch's signature (the reliquary's recognition, the acolytes' containment doctrine) is *carried intact* into 022b, where it *collides* with the antiquarian branch's signature (the copy, the filed fragment) rather than being re-asserted in isolation. The 040b frontier should then have *temple-specific* available actions (the acolytes' presence, the reliquary's seam) that the antiquarian route does not have.
- **The 040a witness fork (commit/refuse) is the protected element** (candidate law `law_bb750d1bdb10`). The mutation must not touch the causal ownership of the decisive turn (the protagonist's act causes the recall; the system's response is a new method, not a reversion). It should *extend* the 040a fork's state difference downstream: the "commit" branch and the "refuse" branch should carry different *causal state* into their respective frontiers (040a-make-the-witness-work vs. 040g), not just different *objects* (subject vs. lever spent).

**The structural lens: branch consequence + reconvergence collision.** Not scene order, not revelation timing, not who is forced to act — those are downstream of the branch-consequence change. The mutation's *objective* is to make the story's forks *causally* different (not presentationally different), so that the reconvergence nodes have something genuinely new to produce, and the "still" chains, the route-bleed, the single-exit corridors, and the 021b teleport all resolve as *consequences* of the fix rather than requiring their own separate mutations.
