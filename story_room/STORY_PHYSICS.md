# Voyd Story Physics

The Voyd Terminal is governed by recursive dramatic dialectic, not by mood, lore density, or beat-sheet compliance.

## Prime law: every dramatic unit changes state

Every meaningful unit has three movements:

1. **Claim / desire / pressure** — somebody asserts, wants, attempts, asks, attacks, withholds, offers, or commits.
2. **Counterforce** — another will, fact, cost, obstacle, contradiction, or consequence resists it.
3. **Turn / synthesis** — the collision creates a new state. The new state is not a compromise; it is what becomes true because the collision happened.

If movement 3 does not change what can happen next, the unit is decorative.

This law is recursive:

`line -> exchange -> beat -> scene -> sequence -> act -> whole story`

The larger unit must be produced by the collisions inside it, not pasted on top as a label.

## Dialogue: tennis, not transcription

Every line is an action. A speaker wants a response or effect. The other speaker blocks, redirects, exposes, counters, accepts at a cost, or changes the terms. The exchange ends somewhere neither line could have reached alone.

Reject dialogue when lines can be reordered without changing the scene; when both speakers merely state theme; when a response does not answer the pressure of the prior line; or when subtext never changes tactics.

## Beat

A beat begins when a tactic is active and ends when resistance forces a tactic, objective, status, knowledge, or risk to change. A beat that only adds imagery is not a beat.

## Scene

A scene must contain:

- an immediate objective
- concrete opposition
- escalating tactics or pressure
- a gap between expectation and result
- a turn or reversal caused by action
- a value change from opening to closing
- an observable consequence inherited by the next scene

Useful craft lenses: McKee's value change, progressive complication, and expectation/result gap; Truby's desire/opponent/plan/revelation; Egri's premise expressed through conflict; Swain's goal/conflict/disaster and reaction/dilemma/decision. These are lenses, not templates.

## Sequence

Scenes in a sequence must argue with each other. Scene B is not simply later than Scene A; it should complicate, negate, reinterpret, exploit, or force payment for Scene A. The sequence ends when the protagonist's strategy is no longer sufficient.

## Act

Each act is itself a three-movement dramatic unit:

- establish a governing strategy/value/worldview
- subject it to an opposing force that exposes its insufficiency
- force a commitment, reversal, or synthesis that makes the next act necessary

## Whole-story transformation

Use Vogler/Campbell as a transformation map, not a mandatory twelve-box checklist. Thresholds, tests, ordeal, reward, road back, resurrection, and return matter only when they mark changes in identity, obligation, knowledge, or action. A stage name earns nothing by itself.

The story must embody an argument. The ending answers the central dramatic question through irreversible action, not explanation.

## Interactive drama

A player choice is dramatic only if each option represents a different action under pressure and creates a distinguishable consequence. Branches may reconverge, but the state carried into convergence must differ.

A choice is fake if the next scene would be substantially identical after swapping the labels.

## Canon rule

There are two kinds of canon:

- **Source canon**: facts from The Gate of Nyandor books and author-approved mythography. Agents may not contradict or silently rewrite it.
- **Terminal story canon**: the current dramatic spine, scene facts, relationships, setups, payoffs, and consequences of the Voyd Terminal. The Structural Editor may revise this when evidence from the room shows the story is failing.

Canon material is ammunition, not an obligation. Never insert a source-canon event merely because it is unused or high-tension. It enters only when the current causal chain creates a need for it.

## Anti-sludge law

Reject scenes whose primary accomplishment is any of the following:

- a striking image
- lore delivery
- generalized darkness, hunger, blood, bones, doors, ash, or cosmic language
- an aphorism
- an unexplained proper noun
- a numerical tension increase
- a structural label such as "turn" or "choke" without an actual change in dramatic state

Specific action outranks atmosphere. Causality outranks surprise. Consequence outranks intensity.
