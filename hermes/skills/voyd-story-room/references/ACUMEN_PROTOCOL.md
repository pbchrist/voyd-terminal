# Acumen / Story Genome Protocol

The proprietary asset is not a prompt. It is the accumulated record of Patrick's structural judgment and the machinery that learns when that judgment applies.

## Acumen Keeper

The Acumen Keeper never writes story prose and never votes on taste. It watches structural decisions, especially Patrick's selections at unresolved forks, and maintains `story_room/genome.json`.

For every human selection it records:

- the fork and all surviving mutations
- what Patrick selected
- Patrick's stated reason, in his words when possible
- the structural distinction that appears to govern the choice
- any proposed reusable law
- whether that law was explicit or inferred

Explicit laws become active immediately. Inferred laws remain candidates until a second independent Patrick decision supports the same law. One ambiguous choice is not doctrine.

## Mutation, not rewrite

When the room finds a wound, the Structural Editor produces 2-4 structurally distinct mutations. A mutation changes dramatic machinery, not adjectives.

Useful mutation dimensions include objective, opposition, tactic, revelation timing, power/status, causal ownership, price of success, branch consequence, scene order, withheld option, setup/payoff placement, and who must act.
Each mutation states:

- what structural fact changes
- what new causal chain follows
- what the protagonist/player gains
- what it costs
- what downstream scenes must now obey
- which active Genome laws it inherits or appears to violate

## Selection hierarchy

1. Canon/Continuity Steward blocks impossible mutations.
2. Phantom Walkers walk every surviving mutation independently.
3. A demonstrated structural failure eliminates a mutation; no numeric average can rescue it.
4. Story Genome hard laws may eliminate mutations already decided by Patrick's prior judgment.
5. If exactly one mutation remains, the system may select it autonomously.
6. If multiple genuinely different, structurally viable futures remain and inherited law does not decide them, this is a **speciation gate** and belongs to Patrick.

## Human speciation gate

Do not send Patrick pages of prose. Present the irreducible decision in compact form, usually 2-4 options:

`A — structural change -> story consequence -> price`

Ask for a selection and, when useful, one sentence of why. The explanation is more valuable than a rating.
## Inheritance

After Patrick chooses, the Acumen Keeper records the decision, proposes or confirms Genome laws, and gives the selected mutation to the Dramatist. Future rooms load active laws before mutation and selection.

The system should bother Patrick less over time, not more. Repeated decisions become inherited acumen. Novel artistic forks still come home to him.

## Negative knowledge

Rejections are inherited too. Preserve what characteristic was rejected, not merely which candidate lost. Rejected material may later contribute a different mechanism through recombination, but a known rejected structural flaw must not silently re-enter.

## Recombination

A future Structural Editor may combine compatible mechanisms from previously rejected mutations when doing so creates a new causal species. Recombination must still pass walkers, canon prosecution, and Genome law.
