# Book 1: The Furbidden Voyd

## Chapter I: The Tail's End?
> "Every cat stands at a fork in the road. It is the curse of our kind to remember the path not taken and to wonder, forever, if it led to a better place." — H.R. Nimblequick

Sory'n wakes inside a black expanse — possibly the Voyd, possibly the afterlife (Aurathel/Cathenna). No guiding light, no spirit guardians. Pure darkness.

## Chapter II: Fear and Desire
> "A kit is born with two hungers. Milk and praise. The first is easily sated. The second can devour an entire life."

Professor Axmar addresses Initiates in The Amethyst Atrium. Sory'n's mocha swirls tighten with anxiety.

## Chapter III: The Proving Ground
> "The Assessment does not test how an Initiate will perform. It tests where an Initiate will break."

The Assessment at the Proving Ground. Four megalithic amethyst lingams. Sory'n faces her trial under immense pressure.

## Chapter IV: Lower Than Low
> "To understand the truth of a city, do not look to its palaces. Look to its gutters."

P'taxx's marginalia from the Guild-led purge. Life in the Lower City.

## Chapter V: Catophilus and M'areta
> "We are taught to dream of a pairing of the heart. We learn to find purpose in a pairing of houses."

Queen M'areta's private counsel on political marriage and power dynamics.

## Chapter VI: The Furnal Equinox
> "A cat's true darkness is laid bare in the shadowless moment."

The Furnal Equinox event. Sory'n's internal darkness revealed.

## Chapter VII: The Wetter nose imbibarium
> "Curiosity is a door that opens inward. But be warned. The things that lurk on the other side are often curious, too."

Investigation and discovery. The dangers of looking too closely.

## Chapter VIII: The Common Spark
> "A tyrant's power is a dam built of fear. It can hold back a river for lifetimes, but it cannot stop the rain."

**Pivotal chapter.** Mother Ertree reveals the truth: "All cats are magical. Every single one." Sory'n realizes her uncle P'taxx wasn't mad — he was a prophet. The common spark is truth, not heresy. This knowledge would destroy the Guild forever.

## Chapter IX: Slight Return
> "Order is not a natural state. It is a garden, maintained by sharp claws and the ruthless pruning of weeds."

P'halyn's personal diary. Return to the city and its consequences.

## Chapter X: Something Out of Nothing
> "There is a fine line between correcting a flaw in Leoran's design and claiming the role of designer for oneself."

Denidrata Sol's diary. Conjuration magic and the Voyd's power to create from nothing.

## Chapter XI: Breaking Free
> "A true awakening is not a gentle dawn. It is the violent shattering of the world you knew."

Sory'n's awakening and break from the Guild system.

## Chapter XII: My Ember
> "A cat does not choose a familiar. A familiar is the shape of a cat's soul when it finally learns its own name."

Sory'n's familiar bond forms — Ember the fire panther.

## Chapter XIII: Stray No More
> "The greatest truths do not announce themselves with fanfare. They arrive as a quiet purr."

P'taxx's statement to the Inquest of Heresies. Integration with the forest clowder.

## Chapter XIV: Fear-Eater
> "Trust is the currency of fools and the bait of the patient hunter."

Alaric R'ast's surveillance report. Fear-Eater and the Ferals.

## Chapter XV: The Furbidden Voyd
> "We believe our choices weave the threads of our destiny, forgetting that the loom was built by a will far greater than our own."

Sory'n discovers and begins wielding the Voyd magic. The forbidden force that shattered the Mewniverse.

## Chapter XVI: The Circle of Fate
> "The clowder seeks to survive the storm. The feral seeks to become it."

The clowder faces the storm. Fear-Eater's philosophy.

## Chapter XVII: A Path Diverged
> "Heresy is not born from malice, but from the sincere belief that one's own paws can correct the flaws in Leoran's Mewniverse."

Orachys' annotation on P'taxx's heresy — bitter pride of a cat trying to prove he's more powerful. Sory'n witnesses common cats using magic openly: "The heresy of the common spark was no longer a heresy. It was a way of life."

## Chapter XVIII: Lost and Found

Sory'n is pulled through the Voyd. The world dissolves. Ember sacrifices herself, joining with the Obsidian Beast. Sory'n's reality rewrites.
