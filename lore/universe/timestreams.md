# Timestreams & Multiverse

## The Nature of Timestreams

Multiple parallel timestreams exist simultaneously — a vast web of glowing filaments pulsing with unidirectional flow. Sory'n witnesses this firsthand when she enters the Voyd: "An array of glowing filaments wove around and through each other... Timestreams. Incomprehensible awe seized her. Tears streamed from her wide-open eyes, shed not for sadness, but for the sheer, crushing scale of it."

The web isn't just external — it's her. The Voyd reveals that all timelines exist at once within the mind of Leoran, the Creator.

## Named Timestreams

- **Timestream III** — Referenced in Ertree's closing quote: "We are the weavers and the thread, the singers and the song. In the silence of the Voyd, our shared breath is the only star."
- **Timestream VI** — Referenced in Orachys's Personal Memoirs quote: "The universe bends to my design. The Voyd itself shall be my signature."

## The Divergent Timeline

Sory'n's Voyd portal at the Ashai wellspring fractures reality, creating a new timeline. In this divergent path:

- Magic is common knowledge — the Common Spark is openly practiced
- The Guild's control has collapsed; magic is taught openly
- The Acatdemy has become a legitimate school, not a gatekeeping institution
- Technology advanced differently
- Sory'n secured her Guild membership (though she later regrets it)

Key quote from C.A.T.: "The Voyd has gathered so much power throughout the span of all the tellings that it has begun to warp all the timestreams. I feel it reaching back, grabbing her by the scruff, and pulling her, yet again, down the path that leads to the end of the world."

## Voyd Gravity

The Voyd possesses gravity — it warps timestreams, pulling everything toward its own inevitable end. C.A.T. describes it as "gravity" rather than chance: "It feels like there is more than mere chance at work this time. It feels like... gravity."

The Voyd became self-aware through Sory'n's choices, serving as Book 2's narrator. It speaks "the language of magnetism. Of inevitability."

## Timeline Mechanics

- Time flows one-way only: "Time flows but one way. But a Voyd portal would let you reach back to any point and change a single thread."
- Regrets represent alternate paths: "Regrets are the ghosts of timelines we didn't choose."
- Changing an aspect creates imbalance, which can be self-corrected: "To change an aspect of a timeline, it could be argued, creates an imbalance. However, beseeching it for 'Holy balance' could be a way for it somehow self-correct."

## Temporal Recursion

The reader experiences "temporal recursion" — a chaotic interval where canonical history rewrites itself in real-time. The reader is positioned as a "Construct" keeping the Mewniverse alive through engagement. The transition is described as "not clean."

## The Mewniverse

Destroyed in Second Era 2321 when Denidrata Sol's Voyd portal swallowed the entire timeline. Now exists only within the narrative itself. C.A.T.'s claim: "For the Mewniverse to live again, its tale must find a worthy listener."
