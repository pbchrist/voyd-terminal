# The Mewniverse & Nyandor

## The Mewniverse

A transdimensional construct that originally existed within the mind of **Leoran** (the Creator). It contained multiple realms, with Nyandor being the central feline-dominated world.

- **Destruction:** In Second Era 2321, the Mewniverse was shattered and reduced to an echo. It now exists only within the narrative itself — sustained by the act of storytelling.
- **Restoration:** The Mewniverse can potentially be restored if the tale finds "worthy listeners." The reader's engagement with the story literally keeps the universe alive.

## Nyandor

- **Geography:** Nine continents
- **Capital:** Faelspire (The Eternal Catpital)
- **Major locations:** Forest Primeval, Mount Sylvest, Aard Voreath Templu, Golden Stairway, Proving Ground, Ebon Heights, Lower City, Wellspring

### Faelspire (The Eternal Catpital)

- Walled city of multi-story stone structures with pitched tile roofs
- Located between the Forest Primeval and Mount Sylvest
- **Three suns:** Sauriat (orange, largest), Megolon, and Nartai (smallest)
- **Diamond Bloom:** Celestial phenomenon when all three suns align, creating diamond-shaped shadows
- **Districts:**
  - Acatdemy Road — Guild district
  - Ebon Heights — Hilltop enclave of the wealthy
  - Felixia Street — Tavern district
  - Lower Commerce — Merchant quarter
  - Eastclaw — Hardscrabble neighborhood
  - Lower City — Where the marginalized live

### Mount Sylvest

- Towering mountain overlooking Faelspire
- Icy K'otan summit
- Contains the **Golden Stairway** (7,000 steps carved into the south face)
- Leads to the **Proving Ground** high above the city

### Forest Primeval

- Vast forest adjacent to Faelspire
- Home to the **Strays** (outlaw cats living outside the Guild system)
- Contains the **Wellspring** (origin point of magical energy)

### Key Locations

- **Aard Voreath Templu:** Amethyst ziggurat at Mount Sylvest base, seat of the Magical Guild
- **The Acatdemy:** Guild training academy within the Templu
- **The Proving Ground:** Assessment site high above Faelspire with four megalithic amethyst lingams
- **Golden Stairway:** 7,000 steps carved into Mount Sylvest, climbed by Initiates on Assessment Day
- **Tol Manor:** The Archmage's estate in Ebon Heights
- **The Ebonwood:** Location of significant artifact/portal
- **The Catservatory:** Theater/performance venue
- **Wellspring:** Origin point of magical energy in the Forest Primeval
