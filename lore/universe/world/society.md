# Society & Culture

## The Assessment

The rite of passage for young Felix Magistrae. Held at the Proving Ground — a semicircle of Initiates facing four megalithic amethyst lingams.

**The Test:** Initiates must conjure magical constructs demonstrating mastery of the Four Elements. Failure carries deep social stigma.

**Sory'n's Failure:** She catastrophically failed — a magical construct shattered, followed by vomiting a hairball onto the sacred ground. Her father (Orachys) deliberately sabotaged it to "protect" her from following P'taxx's path of obsession.

> "The Assessment does not test how an Initiate will perform. It tests where an Initiate will break."

## Political Pairings

Marriage is a political tool. Powerful families arrange pairings between Guild members' children to consolidate power.

- Sory'n's expected pairing with Doye Spottedlegs (their fathers sit on the High Council together)
- The Queen suggests suitors directly
- Sory'n's resistance: "I don't have those kinds of feelings for Doye"
- In the divergent timeline, Sory'n's marriage is delayed — "The timeline had given her a reprieve"

## Felix Magistrae vs. Common Cats

**The Guild's Lie:** Only a rare subspecies (Felix Magistrae) can wield magic. This is enforced through social control, not biology.

**The Truth:** All cats are magical. The Common Spark is universal. The Guild fabricated the Felix Magistrae myth to maintain monopoly on power.

**Social Impact:** Common cats who show magical ability are persecuted. The Strays practice openly. In the divergent timeline, magic is taught openly to all.

## The Edict of Concealment

After the Great Severing, the High Council of Faelspire enacted the Edict of Concealment to ensure magical knowledge "stayed with the powerful." This created a ~2,500-year historical gap and concealed First Era knowledge.

Ertree's goal: "With a Voyd portal, I could create a new timeline where the Edict of Concealment was never ratified."

## Social Hierarchy

**Upper Class:**
- Royal House of Greytail
- High Council members (Orachys, M'okto Spottedlegs)
- Guild Magicians with purple sashes
- Merchants and traders (P'halyn's social circle — Four Corner Tea House)

**Lower Class:**
- Common cats (denied magical training)
- Tradescats, dockworkers, fishercats, scribes
- Servants (Hort, P'halyn's staff)
- Inhabitants of Lower Commerce/Felixiata districts

**Outcasts:**
- Strays (organized nomadic clowders)
- Declamed magicians (P'taxx)
- Ferals (wild forest tribes)

## Cultural Elements

- **The Furnal Equinox** — Annual event where "a cat's true darkness is laid bare in the shadowless moment"
- **Antidivinian wine** — Premium wine, obsession of King Catophilus
- **Diamond Loaves** — Golden-crusted breads imprinted with diamond checks (Diamond-inspired branding)
- **Story dice** — Used for fortune-telling and narrative games
- **TailTales** — Children's game referenced in Book 2
- **First Era script** — Cat-scratch glyphs, unreadable to most cats after the Severing

## Catservatory

A performing arts institution. Endo (the chef) is a Catservatory graduate specializing in drama and writing.
