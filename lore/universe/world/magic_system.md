# Magic System

## Voreath (The Common Spark)

The primary magic system, based on intention and emotional states.

- **The Lie:** Only a tiny fraction of cats can wield it (**Felix Magistrae**). This is fabricated by the Guild to maintain monopoly on magic.
- **The Truth:** All cats are magical. Every single one. The Common Spark is universal. The Guild restricts access through social control, not biology.
- Requires entering the **Null State** (meditative trance)
- **Constructs:** Mental visualization realms where intention becomes substance
- **Pawsitions:** Specific paw movements/gestures for different spells
- **Intention Principle:** Selfless intention = stronger magic; selfish intention = constricted/severed magic
- **Familiars:** Magical companions (like Ember the fire panther) born from gratitude. Not conjured through standard pawsitions. They emerge from the deepest emotion a cat is feeling.
- **Wellsprings:** Places where the Nyand breathes. In these places, pawsitions are useless — the magic flows directly through the cat without formal gestures. Sory'n discovers this from the wizened cat (Cazza'nha) at the Imbibarium.
- **Conjuration:** Creating something from nothing through pawsitions and energy manipulation

## The Four Elements (The Tetrad)

1. **Nyandimancy (Earth):** Manipulation of terra, rocks, ground
2. **Aeromancy (Air):** Wind currents, flying, air manipulation
3. **Pyromancy (Fire):** Flame, heat, combustion
4. **Hydromancy (Water):** Water, liquid manipulation

**Elemental Fusion:** Combining elements is heresy in Guild doctrine. The Strays practice it openly (e.g., the Obsidian Beast was a fusion of all four elements). Sory'n wonders if her Voyd conjuration changed the Guild's stance on fusion.

## The Forbidden Voyd

The most potent arcane force in the Mewniverse. Not an element — a dimension of pure potential.

- **What it is:** A way to reach back in time and change a single thread, rewinding everything downstream. "Imagine reaching into the mind of Leoran himself and reweaving the threads of time."
- **How it works:** Requires a wellspring (a place where the Nyand breathes). Father fails 694,000+ times because he's doing it in his laboratory, not at a wellspring. The charcoal drawing in P'taxx's forbidden tome shows the ritual was performed at an outcropping with an Ashai tree.
- **Denidrata Sol's disaster:** He created something from nothing, wished to prevent the war with the Tol Dominion, but his wife didn't recognize him. He tried to fix the timeline, made it worse, and the Voyd consumed the world.
- **Orachys's obsession:** Conjures a marble-sized black singularity that appears then collapses. He catalogs each attempt with six-digit numbers and two meandering traces showing the path his paws took through 3D space.
- **Sory'n's success:** At the twisted Ashai wellspring, she enters the Voyd and creates a tiny black disc (not a sphere). It persists. It has gravity. It pulls.
- **Unique characteristic:** The Voyd becomes self-aware through the choices of those who wield it. It has gravity — it warps timestreams, pulling everything toward its own inevitable end. C.A.T. fears it has gathered enough power across all tellings to warp reality itself.

## The Heresy of the Common Spark

**Definition:** "All cats are magical. Every single one."

- Not just the Felix Magistrae, but every cat is a vessel of power the Guild spent generations trying to smother
- P'taxx of Tol discovered this — the "flickers of magic in common cats"
- P'taxx was Declaimed (not drowned, as previously stated) for propagating this knowledge
- Orachys viewed P'taxx's pursuit as "a heresy of biology, not cosmology" — chasing minnows instead of the Voyd (the ocean)
- By Book 1's end, Sory'n witnesses common cats using magic openly: "The heresy of the common spark was no longer a heresy. It was a way of life."
- In the divergent timeline (Book 2), the Common Spark is public knowledge. The Guild's control has collapsed. Magic is taught openly. The Acatdemy has become a legitimate school, not a gatekeeping institution.
