# History

## First Era

Ancient period of Nyandor before the Great Severing. Cats practiced magic openly. The Guild existed solely to catalog advancements. First Era script was the written language — cat-scratch glyphs etched into stone and parchment. Almost all First Era knowledge was lost or concealed after the Severing.

Key figure: **Denidrata Sol**, the last Archmage of the First Era. Obsessed with Voyd magic, believed it was real, and drove himself mad attempting to create a Voyd portal. His diary survived (pages hidden in a vault beneath the Templu Archives, though replaced with blanks by someone with access).

## The Great Severing

**What the Guild teaches:** Selfish magicians angered Leoran; He tore reality apart as punishment, restricting magic to the rare Felix Magistrae subspecies.

**What actually happened:** Denidrata Sol created a Voyd portal to rewrite history — specifically to prevent King Registus's war with the Tol Dominion. The portal worked initially but caused cascading unintended changes (his wife stopped recognizing him). He tried to fix his timeline by making increasingly specific wishes through the portal. The more energy he poured in, the more chaotic the changes became. The portal grew until it swallowed the entire timeline, shattering the Mewniverse.

**Aftermath:** Survivors didn't lose magic — they concealed it. The Magical Guild of Voreath reformed, repackaging magic as a tool of war ("defense"), and fabricated the Felix Magistrae myth: a "rare subspecies" almost exclusively found in powerful families. In truth, every cat harbors the Common Spark, but the Guild hid it to maintain control.

The Great Severing created a ~2,500-year historical gap. First Era script became unreadable to most cats. The Guild's cover-up was so complete that almost no one questioned the official narrative.

## Second Era

### The Guild's Control
- **Templu:** The ziggurat at the foot of Mount Sylvest, housing the Archives and the Nekonomicon (3,000-year-old catalog of Voreath evolution)
- **High Council:** Rules through fear and manufactured scarcity of magic
- **Kiton:** Secret police force, led by Commander Alaric R'ast
- **Acatdemy:** Magical academy on Mount Sylvest where Felix Magistrae are identified and trained

### The Strays' Resistance
- Organized nomadic clowders living in the Forest Primeval
- Use First Era tunnels beneath cities for movement and supply runs
- Led by Mother Ertree, who has led the movement for nearly 2,500 years
- Goal: awaken all cats to their magical heritage and destroy the Guild's monopoly

### Key Events (Books 1 & 2)
- **Sory'n's Assessment Failure:** Orachys deliberately sabotaged it to protect her from the same obsession that destroyed P'taxx
- **Sory'n discovers the Strays:** Finds Mother Ertree in the Forest Primeval; learns the Common Spark heresy is true
- **Denidrata Sol's diary stolen:** Sory'n retrieves it from her father's laboratory and delivers it to Mother Ertree
- **Alaric attacks the clowder:** Uses Sory'n as bait to track down Mother Ertree's camp. Deploys 150-200 soldiers. The clowder creates an Obsidian Beast (collective conjuration) but is overwhelmed
- **Mother Ertree captured:** Surrenders herself to save the clowder
- **Iatris killed:** In a final battle with Alaric R'ast atop the promontory
- **Sory'n creates a Voyd portal:** At the Ancient Ashai wellspring, using gratitude instead of arrogance. Rewrites history
- **Timeline fracture:** Creates a divergent reality where magic is common, the Guild never existed, and technology advanced differently
