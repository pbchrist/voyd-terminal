## Core Premise

A metafictional saga set in the **Mewniverse** — a feline-dominated universe destroyed in Second Era 2321, now existing only within the narrative itself. Framed by **C.A.T.** (The Creator of All Tales), who claims storytelling can restore the Mewniverse if it finds "worthy listeners."

Follows **Sory'n of Tol**, Archmage's daughter, as she discovers forbidden Voyd magic and reshapes reality. Book 1 follows her arc; Book 2 shifts narration to the Voyd entity itself as it gains self-awareness.

This franchise is a **fortress against the Nothing** — a narratively sovereign universe that requires active participation to survive. It cannot be replicated by AI because it is built on human agency, community, and genuine choice.
