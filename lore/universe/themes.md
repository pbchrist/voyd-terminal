# Themes

1. **Identity** — Who am I beyond my bloodline/expectations?
2. **Power** — Authority, control, who holds it and why
3. **Freedom** — Escape from destiny, breaking free
4. **Truth** — Secrets, revelation, what is hidden
5. **Destiny** — Fated paths vs. self-determined choices
6. **Storytelling** — Stories as reality-shaping force
7. **Family** — Blood ties, pressure, expectations
8. **Sacrifice** — What must be given up for restoration
9. **Narrative Sovereignty** — The fight against AI and corporate homogenization. Living a story so distinct that no algorithm can collapse it.
10. **The Light Test** — The universe rewards characters who exercise free will under adversity. Making the hard choice generates "light."
11. **Authorship** — We are not just characters in a script; we are the authors of our own lives. The universe loves stories where characters exercise agency.
