# City Allies & Press

## Acatdemy & Allies

- **Leba Whitebelly** — Black-and-white shorthair with lime-green eyes. Authoritative, pragmatic, serious. Model Guild aspirant and Sory'n's closest friend.
- **Chartru'el Duskyfur** — Philosophical silver-gray tom with white bib. Noble lineage, inclined to pretension. Core member of Sory'n's inner circle.
- **Doye Spottedlegs** — Sharp-witted, freethinking tabby tom, quietly fond of Sory'n. Son of Guild Council's second-most powerful member.
- **M'okto Spottedlegs** — Guardian of Arcane Secrets. Father to Doye. Rigid doctrinaire and Orachys' fiercest political rival.

## The Faelspiran Hornblower (Press)

- **Art Cattinson** — Beat reporter. Hungry for stories with teeth. Relentless curiosity is newsroom legend.
- **Maidencoat Spur** — Typesetter and cub reporter. Manages presses, shares Cattinson's hunger for big scoop.
- **"Arcane" Arthur Tuftwallow** — Head of Research. Walking archive with weakness for obscure.
- **Jehos'a Yellowtail** — Editor-In-Chief. Pragmatist walking razor's edge between truth and offending the Crown.
