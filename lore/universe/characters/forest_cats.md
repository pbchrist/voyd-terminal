# Forest Cats (The Strays)

Outlaw magicians operating outside Guild sanction in the Forest Primeval. Use First Era tunnels beneath cities for movement and supply runs. Practice magic communally, with Familiars openly.

## Mother Ertree (P'thai En'ta Ertree)

The leader of the Strays. Dense marbled coat of gold and black with wild, untamed symmetry. Broad rosettes spiral across her flank. Shaggy white muzzle. Heterochromatic eyes — one piercing viridian, one fathomless blue. Voice hums low and steady, resonating in the chest.

**History:** Former Guild member. Classmate of Orachys and P'taxx at the Academy. Both brothers courted her; she chose P'taxx. After P'taxx's declamation, she became the leader of the Strays and has led the resistance for nearly her entire life.

**Magic:** Extraordinarily powerful. Can levitate the clowder using the collective will of all her cats. Has a failing heart that requires Wellsprings to survive.

**Relationship with P'taxx:** Deep, enduring love. They nuzzle and purr when reunited. She calls him "Old friend" and "miraculous one." Their bond is the emotional center of the resistance.

**Key quotes:** "You opened it. You must find a way to close it." / "Unlike traditional magic, which channels what exists, Voyd magic rewrites it. It reaches into Leoran's mind, where infinite possibilities exist at once, and collapses them into a single chosen outcome."

## Iatris

Mother Ertree's advisor and bodyguard. Hairless Sphynx. Lean, long-limbed, ancient in posture. Cold, assessing gaze. Fierce fighter. Ertree's romantic partner — Ertree grooms her and calls her "my sweet love."

**Death:** Killed by Alaric R'ast atop the promontory. She rises "like a specter" and delivers a final swipe to Alaric's throat after the portal collapses.

**Key trait:** Does not trust Sory'n initially. "She's not your mother." Deeply protective of Ertree.

## Cazza'nha

The wizened, coon-like elder. Magnificent salt-and-pepper ruff. Ancient moss-agate eyes. Master Hydromancer — pulls moisture from humid air into drenching waves. Speaks with authority: "There are places in the Primeval Forest where the Nyand breathes."

**Death:** Killed during the final battle. Cornered against an Ebontree by three soldiers. Takes one down with a burst of white fire before the other two find his heart. Dead before he hits the ground. Y'ond carries his body in his forelegs during the retreat.

## Y'ond

Wiry orange tabby with dirtied white muzzle, twitchy ears, cunning ruffled face. Former bandit who turned Stray. Wily expression. Master Aeromancer — creates violent gusts of wind and a massive dust storm during the final battle.

**Familiar:** Ciro, an invisible Nyandimantic dog, visible only through dirt clinging to his form. Represents what Y'ond was missing: loyalty.

**Key trait:** Paranoid but brilliant. His eyes are used in the silent signal chain that coordinates the Strays during the final battle.

## V'dath

Dusky blue female Aeromancer. Pulls her cowl low. Quiet, observant.

## T'resor

Corpulent calico with Nyand magic mastery. Can levitate plants.

## Lyra

Smoky-faced tom with folded-down ears and a short tail. Killed by Alaric while protecting Titi.

**Familiar:** Quilliam, a fire porcupine that can be hijacked between casters.

## Endo

Round-faced chef with a flat nose. Water mage. Catservatory graduate specializing in drama and writing.

## Fery'n

Older white-muzzled brown tabby. Decennial merit scholar. Pyromancer. Former Guild member who fled. Dies during the battle.

## Jit'ra

Silver cat with shimmering coat and mirrored eyes that catch glints of pink and gold. Kindred spirit to Sory'n. Lost her daughter Y'una during their city escape.

## No-Nose

Young black cat with a black nose.

## Hema

Tabby kitten with affinity for fire magic.

## Titi

Once-white puffball kitten. Born in a wellspring. Lyra's daughter.

## D'yman

Lean sentry.

## Fear-Eater

Leader of the Ferals (wild forest cats allied with the Strays). Scarred black tom with a raspy growl and predatory demeanor. Philosophy: consuming fear makes you stronger.

## The Ferals

Wild forest tribes that sometimes ally with the Strays. Fight alongside them in the final battle.

## The Clowder

A nomadic magical community — not a fixed camp. Mobile, hidden, constantly moving. They practice magic openly: drawing symbols in dirt, levitating leaves, whispering to Familiars. Multiple pantherine Familiars move through the clearing, each distinct in hue and glow.
