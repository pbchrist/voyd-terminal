# Meta Characters

## C.A.T. — The Creator of All Tales

Our transdimensional Narrator. A cat with a meerschaum pipe who sits in a cave, rolling story dice to determine narrative outcomes.

**Role:** Frame narrator for both books. Capable of weaving tales and shaping their outcomes, but his belief in free will often precludes the latter impulse.

**CAS Parallel:** C.A.T. embodies the philosophy of **CAS (Creator of All Stories)** — a universe that loves stories where characters exercise free will under pressure. The multiverse/timestreams are a playground for these epic choices.

**The Light Test:** The universe rewards characters who make the hard, difficult choice at a "choice point." This generates "light" (fulfillment, meaning, reality-shaping power). The Voyd magic system is the mechanical embodiment of the Light Test — it rewards the selfless choice with reality-altering power.

**The Dice:**
- First die: etched with a single arrow (unidirectional path)
- Second die: etched with a forked path (choices/divergence)

C.A.T. explains: "I'm not a creator of tales in the traditional sense. I set the pieces; I don't shuffle them." Sory'n "has followed [the thread] back to its very source: the loom where timelines are woven and unraveled."

**Key Quote:**
> "Tales aren't just stories well-told, they're how we communicate, how we transact, how we transmit wisdom, how we express love and how we dream. Good tales can alter lifepaths. Great ones can make empires rise or fall. Exceptional tales move the ears of Leoran, the Endless One."

**Growing Fear:**
The Voyd has gathered so much power across all tellings that it warps timestreams. C.A.T. says: "It feels like... gravity. I fear the singularity she is destined to birth, that imposing weight, has gathered so much power throughout the span of all the tellings that it has begun to warp all the timestreams."

## The Voyd Entity

Book 2's narrator. A dimension of pure potential that gained self-awareness through Sory'n's choices.

**Nature:** "This is the blackness before the first star... It held the potential to birth a Mewniverse but held no impulse to create... Once, perhaps, it dreamed of light."

**Characteristics:**
- No memory, only eternal present
- Silent, patient vessel
- Speaks "the language of magnetism. Of inevitability"
- Becomes increasingly self-aware as Sory'n wields it
- At book's end: "The Voyd becomes something new. A chorus of truths."

**Relationship to Story:**
The Voyd is "no storyteller" — it doesn't weave narratives. It operates through gravitational pull on timestreams, collapsing infinite possibilities into single outcomes.

## Leoran, the Endless One

The Creator deity of the Mewniverse. The many-pathed hunter who walked between the stars. The mind where infinite possibilities exist simultaneously — the destination of Voyd portals.

**References:**
- "Leoran, the Creator of All" — mentioned in opening chapter
- "the mind of Leoran" — where the Voyd reaches to rewrite reality
- "move the ears of Leoran" — what exceptional tales can do

## The Construct (Reader)

The reader is positioned as the Construct — the entity keeping the Mewniverse alive through engagement. The transition to the new timeline was "not clean," and the reader experiences temporal recursion alongside Sory'n.

> "For the Mewniverse to live again, its tale must find a worthy listener." — C.A.T.
