# Government & Ruling Bodies

## The Magical Guild of Voreath

The most powerful institution in Nyandor, controlling magic, education, and social order.

### Structure

- **High Council of Faelspire** — Rules through fear and manufactured scarcity of magic. Enforced the Edict of Concealment after the Great Severing, ensuring magical knowledge "stayed with the powerful."
- **Acatdemy** — Magical academy on Mount Sylvest where Felix Magistrae are identified and trained. Initiate candidates undergo the Assessment at the Proving Ground.
- **Templu** — Ziggurat housing the Archives and the Nekonomicon (3,000-year-old catalog of Voreath evolution).

### Key Figures

- **Orachys of Tol** — Esteemed Archmage, Architect of Magical Defense for the Realm of Nyandor, Leader of the Guild of Voreath, Lifetime High Council Chair. The most powerful member.
- **M'okto Spottedlegs** — Guardian of Arcane Secrets and father to Doye. Rigid doctrinaire and Orachys's fiercest political rival. Enforces Guild edicts with an iron claw.

### Guild Doctrine

- Only Felix Magistrae can wield magic (a lie — all cats are magical)
- The Edict of Concealment ensures magic remains controlled
- Scholarships are given out "once every ten years to some merchant's kit to prove the system works" — a token gesture
- Guild property includes all Voreathic infusibles (charged magical objects)

## The Greytail Royal House

The reigning monarchs of Nyandor, based in Faelspire.

### King Catophilus Greytail

Corpulent, bombastic ruler known for half-baked decrees and an undying devotion to Antidivinian wine. Established The Sand Club (Members Only) in S.E. 2216. His "gastric arias" (burping) are legendary: "A well-executed eructation is a sign of a truly satisfied monarch!"

### Queen M'areta Greytail

Regal and strategic. Wields keen understanding of political nuance. The King's trusted partner. P'halyn's role model. She offers to intervene in pairings and suggests suitors directly.

### Royal Pairings

Political marriages are a cornerstone of power. The Tol-Greytail dinner is a yearly tradition held on the eve of the Furnal Equinox. The Queen will "suggest suitors herself" — Sory'n's pairing with Doye Spottedlegs is the most likely political match.

## The Royal Kiton

Secret police force of Nyandor.

- **Alaric R'ast** — Head of the Royal Kiton. Cunning and ambitious. Adept hunter and deductive mastermind. Uses surveillance, infiltration, and military force against the Strays. Deploys 150-200 soldiers to attack the clowder.

## City Structure

Faelspire is divided into tiered districts:
- **Commerce District** — Diamond-inspired branding, high-end shops (Four Corner Tea House)
- **West High Street** — Shopping district with lacquered palanquins and heavy traffic
- **Lower Commerce / Felixiata** — Lower-class districts, exposed after the wall breach
- **West End Park** — Public space with rows of resting palanquins
- **Ebon Heights** — Upper-class residential area, home to the wealthy

## Social Classes

Cats of all classes are represented: "kits, tradescats, dockworkers, fishercats, scribes — every class and caste rendered with idealized precision." The Guild and Royal House maintain control through manufactured scarcity and the invisible hierarchies of social geography.
