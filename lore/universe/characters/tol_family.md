# Tol Family

## Sory'n of Tol
Protagonist. Brown-patterned with mocha swirls, white muzzle, white-tipped forelegs. Heir to the most powerful magical bloodline Nyandor has ever seen. Failed her Assessment catastrophically (hairball + vomit). Conjured her first familiar, Ember (a panther), from gratitude. Created a Voyd portal at the wellspring, fracturing the timeline.

## Orachys of Tol (Sory'n's father)
Black fur, obsidian coat, pale hairless scar on left side. Esteemed Archmage of Nyandor. Sabotaged Sory'n's Assessment to "protect" her from following P'taxx's path. His obsession: creating Voyd portals (attempted over 694,000 times). Stole Denidrata Sol's diary from the Templu Archives. His motivation: prove himself superior to P'taxx, achieve mastery over spacetime. In the divergent timeline, becomes even more power-corrupted.

## P'halyn Tol (Sory'n's mother)
Married Orachys for strategic reasons. Once had her own magical spark but deliberately suppressed it to accommodate Orachys. Cold and calculating, but genuinely loves her children. Their marriage was a political deal.

## P'taxx of Tol (Sory'n's uncle)
Orachys's younger brother. Fluffy grey longhair. Former Guild Council member, Declamed for Common Spark heresy (belief that all cats possess magical potential). Both he and Orachys courted Ertree; she chose P'taxx. After declamation, went into hiding. His research into Denidrata Sol's diary inspired Sory'n. Orachys convinced him to recant to save his life, which broke him.

## Y'saris and Myrrha (Sory'n's twin sisters)
Velvety gray kittens. Y'saris is rebellious; Myrrha is perfectionist. Both show magical ability (water conjuration) when Sory'n teaches them.

## Hort
The family's elderly blind housecat. Loyal, long-serving.
