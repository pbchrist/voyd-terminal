# Core Voyd Laws

These are generation constraints distilled from the approved mythography and Gate of Nyandor lore. They are not scene prompts.

## Reality and timestreams

- Many parallel timestreams exist simultaneously as possible histories.
- Time itself flows one way. Voyd magic does not physically send a cat backward in time.
- A Voyd intervention reaches back to a pivotal thread and rewrites the resulting present from that point forward.
- A changed thread propagates consequences through everything downstream. Small changes may create large, unexpected collateral changes.
- Alternate possibilities are not a convenient archive of completed people or lives waiting to be retrieved.

## The Voyd

- The Voyd predates created reality and exists beneath/between the written possibilities of Leoran's creation.
- Voyd magic writes reality; Voreath works with forces already present in reality.
- The Voyd is active rather than neutral: it recruits through curiosity, synchronicity, apparent fate, obsession, and psychological/physical pull.
- The Voyd has gravity across both matter and timestreams. Repeated interactions and directed intention can strengthen its purchase.
- The Voyd is not an ordinary navigable dark dimension and must not become a generic portal realm.

## Will, choice, and consequence

- Directed will collapses possibility toward chosen outcomes. More wills can compound or contest the result.
- Attempts to repair collateral damage by making additional precise changes can create further cascading consequences.
- Genuine release requires unchoosing rather than merely resisting while remaining fixated on an outcome.
- Hard choices under pressure matter cosmologically: free will is a real force in the Mewniverse's narrative metaphysics.
- Every meaningful choice should leave residue in knowledge, relationships, obligations, physical reality, or future possibility.

## Story Room use

Generate new consequences from these laws. Do not merely reenact Sory'n, Orachys, Sol, Ertree, or other book plots under new names.
